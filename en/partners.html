Forms of Cooperation Available

1

Correspondent Counsel

We represent your firm's clients inside Egypt in any legal matter, with regular reporting directly to your office.

2

Reciprocal Case Referrals

Refer Egypt-related matters to us, in exchange for referrals of matters connected to your country to your office when needed.

3

Co-Counsel Representation

Joint work on cross-border matters that require legal expertise from both sides at once.

4

Enforcement of Judgments & Powers of Attorney from Abroad

Enforcement of foreign judgments and decisions before the Egyptian courts, and certification of powers of attorney issued from your country.

5

Comparative Egyptian Law Advisory

Advisory support for your firm on any matter of Egyptian law, for your clients or for research purposes.

6

Research & Knowledge Partnership

Exchange of comparative legal articles and research between the two legal systems, and joint training or lecture opportunities.

7

Civil & Criminal Litigation and Defence

Full litigation and defence before Egyptian courts at every level, in civil and criminal matters of all kinds, for your firm's clients inside Egypt.

8

Representation of Foreign Companies in Egypt

Full legal representation for foreign companies in their dealings, matters, and disputes within the Egyptian market.

The firm also provides distinguished legal care to nationals of brotherly and friendly countries residing inside the Arab Republic of Egypt, through its various activities.

How Cooperation Begins

  1. Contact us and let us know the type of cooperation you'd like to start with (the form below, or directly via WhatsApp/email).
  2. A short introductory call to define the expected scope of work and areas of alignment between our two firms.
  3. Signing a simplified legal cooperation agreement (MOU) defining referral scope, communication process, and fee-sharing.
  4. Work begins on the first referred matter, with a direct point of contact and regular follow-up.
