Public Funds Cases

Public funds cases are among the most delicate and heavily penalized criminal cases. We handle the defense from the first summons to the investigation through to the Court of Cassation.

Contact Us About This Service

The Key Point

These cases usually begin with investigations or lengthy oversight and accounting reports, and their outcome depends on a careful reading of the documents and the expert reports. Many of the most effective lines of defense are built at the investigation stage, not after referral to court.

Common Cases We Handle

  • Embezzlement and misappropriation of public funds, or facilitating their misappropriation.
  • Bribery and acting as an intermediary in it, and abuse of influence.
  • Profiteering from public office.
  • Intentional damage to public funds, and damage through gross negligence.
  • Forgery of official documents and their use.

What We Do

  • Attending the investigation before the Public Prosecution and the Public Funds Prosecution from the first session.
  • Studying the reports of the oversight authorities and experts, and requesting documents and counter-expert committees.
  • Applications for release, and challenges to pretrial detention and to asset-freeze orders.
  • Exploring settlement where the law permits it.
  • Pleading before the Criminal Courts at both levels, and appealing to the Court of Cassation.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do I need a lawyer in a public funds case?

From the very first summons to the investigation. These cases usually begin with investigations or lengthy oversight and accounting reports, and many of the most effective lines of defense are built at the investigation stage, not after referral to court.

What are the most common public funds cases?

Embezzlement and misappropriation of public funds or facilitating their misappropriation, bribery and acting as an intermediary in it, abuse of influence, profiteering from public office, intentional or grossly negligent damage to public funds, and forgery of official documents and their use.

Is settlement possible in public funds cases?

We explore settlement in the cases where the law permits it, and this is determined after studying the file and the reports of the oversight authorities and experts.
