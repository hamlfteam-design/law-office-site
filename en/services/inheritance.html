Inheritance & Estate Division

Dividing an inheritance requires specific procedures before it turns into a dispute between the heirs.

Ask About Your Inheritance File

Basic Steps

  • Obtaining the inheritance declaration and identifying the legal heirs and their shares.
  • Inventorying the estate's funds and property (real estate, accounts, securities, etc.).
  • Dividing the estate amicably among the heirs when they agree, or through a judicial partition suit when they disagree.
  • Registering each heir's share in the real estate according to the official procedures.

For Clients Abroad

If you live abroad and have a share in an inheritance in Egypt, we can follow all procedures on your behalf under a power of attorney certified by the Egyptian consulate, with periodic reports in Arabic or English — without you needing to travel.

Every inheritance case differs according to the number of heirs, the nature of the estate's assets, and the existence of a will — an initial assessment is necessary before determining the right path.
