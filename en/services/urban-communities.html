New Urban Communities Disputes

Is your land or unit in one of the new cities, and has the Authority withdrawn the allocation or refused a license or transfer? We review the decision and act before the deadlines pass.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

The new cities are managed by the New Urban Communities Authority under Law No. 59 of 1979 and its regulations, and its decisions on allocation, withdrawal and licensing are administrative decisions with specific grievance and appeal routes and deadlines. We review the terms booklet, the allocation contract and the correspondence, and pursue a grievance or an appeal before the administrative courts.

What We Do

  • Grievances and appeals against land withdrawal and cancellation of allocation.
  • Requests to extend execution deadlines.
  • Licensing disputes, building violations and their regularization.
  • Transfer of ownership and assignment of land and units.
  • Disputes over installments, fees and late-payment penalties.
  • Representation before the city agencies, the Authority and the State Council courts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who manages Egypt's new cities and how are its decisions challenged?

The New Urban Communities Authority under Law No. 59 of 1979 and its regulations. Its decisions on allocation, withdrawal and licensing are administrative decisions with specific grievance and appeal routes and deadlines.

The Authority withdrew my land or cancelled the allocation — what can I do?

We review the conditions booklet, the allocation contract and correspondence, and file a grievance or a challenge before the administrative courts before the deadlines pass.

Can I request an extension of the execution period?

Yes, requests to extend execution periods are among the matters we handle before the city agencies and the Authority.
