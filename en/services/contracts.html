Contract Drafting & Review

A well-drafted contract prevents disputes before they start. Before you sign, let us review every clause with you.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Most cases that reach the courts begin with a contract written in a hurry or copied from an unsuitable template. We draft the contract to fit your transaction, or review the contract you received, explain the risks and propose amendments — in Arabic, in English, or in a bilingual version.

What We Do

  • Drafting and reviewing sale, lease and construction contracts.
  • Supply, distribution and commercial agency agreements.
  • Partnership and shareholders' agreements.
  • Non-disclosure, confidentiality and non-compete agreements.
  • Bilingual (Arabic/English) contracts for foreign parties.
  • Dispute-resolution clauses: choice of court or arbitration and governing law.

How We Work with You

We begin with a consultation, in person or remotely, followed by a written opinion on your position, then a written fee agreement defining the scope, and periodic written reports as the file progresses. See the full engagement steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why have a lawyer review a contract before signing?

Because most cases that reach the courts start with a contract written in a hurry or copied from an unsuitable template. We explain the risks and propose amendments before you commit.

Do you draft contracts in English?

Yes — in Arabic, in English, or in bilingual versions, especially for foreign parties.

What types of contracts do you draft?

Sale, lease and construction; supply, distribution and commercial agency; partnership and shareholders' agreements; non-disclosure, confidentiality and non-compete agreements; and dispute-resolution and governing-law clauses.

Need advice on your situation?

Send us a summary of the facts and any documents you have, and we will explain your next step — in English or Arabic.
