Customs & Customs Clearance

Is your shipment held at customs, assessed at higher duties than expected, or the subject of a smuggling report? We review the position and take the most suitable legal route with you.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Disputes with customs usually concern the value of the goods, their tariff classification or the documents, and every day of delay adds storage costs and penalties. We review the file quickly and determine whether the answer lies in an administrative grievance, the dispute-resolution committees provided for in Customs Law No. 207 of 2020, or the courts.

What We Do

  • Grievances against customs valuation and tariff classification before the competent committees.
  • Follow-up of customs release and remedying missing documents.
  • Exemption requests and special customs regimes (temporary admission and duty drawback).
  • Defense in customs smuggling cases and settlement requests.
  • Judicial challenges to customs decisions.
  • Reviewing import contracts and delivery terms (Incoterms) for their customs impact.

Frequently Asked Questions

My shipment is held at customs — what should I do?

We review the file quickly and decide whether the solution is an administrative grievance, the dispute-settlement committees under Customs Law No. 207 of 2020, or the courts.

How do I challenge a customs valuation or tariff classification?

Through a grievance against the valuation or classification decision before the competent committees and, where necessary, a challenge before the competent court.

Is settlement possible in customs smuggling cases?

We defend customs smuggling cases and submit settlement requests in them.
