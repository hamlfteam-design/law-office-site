Local Counsel & Partnership for Law Firms

Does your firm have a client who needs representation in Egypt, or a cross-border matter that needs Egyptian counsel? We act as your partner inside Egypt and report to you in writing.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Law firms in Europe and elsewhere often have clients with interests or disputes in Egypt but no trusted counsel on the ground in Cairo to follow the file, appear in court and keep them informed. We act as the Egyptian arm of your firm: the same file, the same standard of follow-up, and written reports in English or Arabic.

Forms of Cooperation

  • Correspondent counsel: we represent your firm's clients in Egypt in any legal proceeding, with periodic direct reports to your firm.
  • Egyptian law opinions: advisory support on any question of Egyptian law, for your clients or for research purposes.
  • Co-counsel: joint work on cross-border matters that need the expertise of both jurisdictions.
  • Enforcement of foreign judgments and arbitral awards before the Egyptian courts, and legalization and use of powers of attorney issued abroad.
  • Reciprocal referrals: Egypt-related files come to us, and files connected to your jurisdiction go to your firm when needed.
  • Representation of foreign companies in Egypt and pleading in civil and criminal cases at every level of litigation.

Why Firms Work With Us

  • A multi-disciplinary team covering the main branches of Egyptian law, with pleading before all levels of courts and the high courts: the Court of Cassation, the Supreme Administrative Court and the Supreme Constitutional Court.
  • An office in central Cairo, close to the principal judicial and government bodies.
  • Regular work with Arab and foreign clients on powers of attorney, inheritance, real estate and enforcement of foreign judgments.
  • A single direct point of contact, and a clear written cooperation agreement before any work begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

What forms of cooperation do you offer foreign and Arab law firms?

Correspondent counsel inside Egypt, mutual referral of matters, co-counsel on cross-border cases, enforcement of foreign judgments and powers of attorney, Egyptian-law advice, and representation of foreign companies in Egypt.

In which language are reports sent to our firm?

Periodic written reports in Arabic or English, with a single direct point of contact.

How do we start working together?

With a clear written cooperation agreement before any work begins. Details and the request form are on our international law-firm cooperation page.

Need advice on your situation?

Send us a summary of the facts and any documents you have, and we will explain your next step — in English or Arabic.
