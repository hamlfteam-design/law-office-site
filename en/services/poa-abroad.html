Power of Attorney from Abroad

Living outside Egypt with a case, property or inheritance inside? You do not have to travel.

Learn the Right Procedure for Your Case

The Idea

All you need is an official power of attorney certified by the Egyptian consulate in your country of residence, and we follow up the remaining procedures inside Egypt on your behalf with periodic written reports.

A point most people get wrong: a general power of attorney is not suitable for everything. Sale has its wording, litigation has its wording, and inheritance has its wording. A power of attorney with the wrong wording means a rejected procedure and months lost.

How We Work with You

  • We determine the correct wording of the power of attorney according to the required procedure (sale, litigation, inheritance, management of funds, etc.).
  • You issue the power of attorney from the Egyptian consulate in your country of residence with the specified wording.
  • We follow the file inside Egypt entirely on your behalf.
  • A periodic written report on every step of the file, in Arabic or English.

We work with clients in the Gulf, Europe and North America, taking time-zone differences into account when scheduling communication.

Need to Issue a Power of Attorney from Abroad?

Send us a summary of your situation, and we will return a guidance memo: the procedure, the authority, the documents, and the expected duration.
