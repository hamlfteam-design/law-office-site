Buying Property in Egypt

A foreigner, expat or Arab buyer considering property in Egypt? We verify the file before you pay, and follow the registration on your behalf.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Buying property from abroad means trusting documents you cannot check yourself. Our role is to be your eyes inside Egypt: we examine the property, the seller and the contract before any transfer of money, and we follow registration and transfer of ownership until the file is closed.

The rules on ownership of property in Egypt by non-Egyptians carry conditions and restrictions that vary by the type and location of the property and the status of the buyer. We check them at the start of the file, before any financial commitment.

What We Do

  • Examine the chain of title and the legal status of the property, including any rights or encumbrances attached to it.
  • Review the purchase contract and draft its terms to protect the buyer, especially payment, delivery and warranty terms.
  • Follow registration and transfer-of-ownership procedures before the competent authorities.
  • Represent you under an official power of attorney if you cannot attend, without traveling. Learn about power of attorney from abroad.
  • Handle sale, inheritance and disputes connected to the property, if the file becomes complicated or a disagreement arises after purchase.

How We Work with You

We begin with a remote consultation, followed by a written opinion on the property and the contract, then a written fee agreement defining the scope. After that you receive periodic written reports in Arabic or English on every step. See the full engagement steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can a foreigner own real estate in Egypt?

Yes. Under Law No. 230 of 1996, a foreigner may own up to two residential properties, each no larger than 4,000 square metres, and may not dispose of them for five years, subject to the exceptions set out in the law.

Can I buy property in Egypt while living abroad?

Yes. We can represent you under a formal power of attorney without you travelling, review the file before you pay, and follow the registration and transfer of title on your behalf, with written reports in Arabic or English.

What is checked before buying a property?

The chain of title and the legal status of the property, whether it carries any rights or obligations, and the purchase contract — especially the payment, delivery and guarantee terms.
