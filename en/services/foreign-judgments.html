Enforcement of Foreign Judgments

A judgment issued outside Egypt and you want to enforce it inside Egypt? The procedure has specific conditions and steps that must be followed in the right order.

Ask About Your File

The Basic Idea

A foreign judgment is not enforced automatically in Egypt. A suit before the Egyptian courts is required to grant it an enforcement order, and to verify that the legal conditions for its enforcement are met — most importantly the condition of reciprocity between Egypt and the country where the judgment was issued, its non-conflict with Egyptian public policy, and the proper notification of the opponent in the original case. The reciprocity condition in particular is the point at which foreign judgments most often stall at enforcement in Egypt, and it must be examined before filing the suit, not after.

General Steps

  • Examining the foreign judgment and confirming it meets the formal and substantive conditions for enforcement in Egypt.
  • Legalization and official translation of the judgment and related documents.
  • Filing the suit for an order to enforce the foreign judgment before the competent court.
  • Following the suit until the enforcement order is issued, then taking the actual enforcement procedures.
The same path applies, with slightly different conditions, to the enforcement of foreign arbitral awards in Egypt.

Who It Is For

People residing abroad who hold a judgment issued by a foreign court and need to enforce it against assets or parties in Egypt, and foreign law firms that want a correspondent lawyer inside Egypt to follow the enforcement file with regular periodic reports.
