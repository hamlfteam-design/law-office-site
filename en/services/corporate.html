Monthly Legal Subscription for Companies

Instead of paying for each problem as it happens, a fixed monthly subscription covers your company's recurring legal needs — designed primarily for small and medium companies that do not need a full-time in-house legal department, with more comprehensive packages for larger companies.

Why a Subscription Instead of Pay-per-Task?

"Legal services for companies" is a generic phrase with no specific value. The monthly legal subscription is a clear product: a published cap of hours, services specified by name, and a fixed price lower than the cost of the same services if billed per task — which is what makes the subscription worth committing to.

What It Covers

  • Drafting and reviewing commercial contracts and employment contracts, commercial agency, commercial representation and franchise contracts.
  • Reviewing disciplinary regulations and non-compete and confidentiality clauses for compliance with the law.
  • Legal follow-up of overdue debt collection.
  • Periodic consultations for managing the company's legal or human-resources affairs, and following labor disputes (contracts, salaries, allowances, termination of service, end-of-service bonus).

Company Formation

Outside the scope of the monthly subscription, we handle the formation of companies in all legal forms: joint-stock, general partnership, limited partnership, partnership limited by shares, and limited liability — with a free legal check-up before formation or partnership.

Expressly excluded from the subscription: case fees, representation before courts at all levels, enforcement, and arbitration — with a subscriber discount on these specified in the contract.

3 Clauses in an Employment Contract That Get Company Owners into Trouble

  • Contract term: A fixed-term contract renews automatically in certain cases and becomes open-ended — and terminating it then becomes an entirely different matter.
  • Penalties clause: If the disciplinary regulation is not approved and announced to the employee, any penalty you impose may have no legal basis.
  • Non-compete and confidentiality clause: If drafted in a general, open-ended way, the courts narrow it or disregard it — it must be defined by activity, duration and scope.

The Most Common Problems Companies Actually Face

  • Late payment: Customers or entities that delay payment with no clear collection or compensation mechanism in the contract.
  • Contracts not drafted in your favor: Ready-made or literally translated contracts that do not comply with Egyptian law and open the door to disputes over scope, penalties or termination.
  • Wrongful dismissal without grounds: Terminating an employee without proper procedures turns the problem into a costly labor lawsuit.
  • Delays in licensing and registration: Delays in formation procedures, licenses or approvals (including free zones) postpone the start of operations.
  • Compliance gaps: Updates to labor, tax or data-protection law surprise small companies that have no in-house legal department.

This is exactly what the monthly subscription covers: contract review at a fixed price before you sign, labor-compliance audits that prevent wrongful-dismissal suits, quick follow-up of formation and licensing procedures, and legal collection of overdue debts.

Packages

The advanced and comprehensive packages suit larger companies with broader or multi-branch operations — details and price are set according to the scale of activity upon contact.
