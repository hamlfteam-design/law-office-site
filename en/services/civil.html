Civil Litigation & Damages

Are you owed money, have you suffered harm from someone's conduct, or do you have an ownership or lease dispute? We identify the right action from the outset.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Civil cases often turn on choosing the right action and presenting the right evidence from the first hearing. We review your documents, determine the most suitable route for your claim, and represent you before the civil courts from first instance to Cassation.

What We Do

  • Claims for material and moral damages.
  • Old-rent and new-rent lease disputes and eviction.
  • Ownership and possession actions, and actions to validate and enforce contracts.
  • Actions to authenticate signatures, monetary claims and payment orders.
  • Rescission of contracts and recovery of sums paid.
  • Appeals and Cassation appeals against civil judgments.

How We Work with You

We begin with a consultation, in person or remotely, followed by a written opinion on your position, then a written fee agreement defining the scope, and periodic written reports as the file progresses. See the full engagement steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

What civil cases do you handle?

Claims for material and moral damages, old and new rent disputes and evictions, ownership and possession actions and validation of contracts, signature validation, monetary claims and payment orders, and rescission of contracts with refund of amounts paid.

Why does choosing the right action matter from the start?

Because civil cases depend heavily on choosing the appropriate action and the correct evidence from the first hearing.

What is the deadline for a Cassation appeal in civil judgments?

Sixty days from the date of the judgment, under Article 252 of the Civil and Commercial Procedure Law.
