Intellectual Property & Trademarks

Has your trademark been copied or registered in someone else's name, or do you want to protect it from day one? We register, protect and defend your intellectual property rights.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Intellectual property rights in Egypt — trademarks, patents, industrial designs and models, and copyright — are governed by Law No. 82 of 2002. Early registration is the foundation of protection, and disputes arising from these rights are heard before the Economic Courts. We start by reviewing your position, then register, follow up and protect.

What We Do

  • Trademark availability searches, filing of registration applications and follow-up.
  • Renewal of registration, and opposition to the registration of similar or imitating marks.
  • Protection of industrial designs and models, and of patents.
  • Copyright protection for works, including software, content and designs.
  • Action against infringement and counterfeiting: warning notices, complaints, and actions for an injunction and damages.
  • Licensing and franchise agreements, and assignment of trademark ownership.
  • Representation before the competent authorities and the Economic Courts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which law governs intellectual property in Egypt?

Law No. 82 of 2002, covering trademarks, patents, industrial designs and copyright. Disputes arising from it are heard by the Economic Courts.

My trademark has been copied — what should I do?

We tackle counterfeiting and infringement through warning letters, complaints, and actions to stop the infringement and claim damages, and we oppose the registration of similar marks.

Why register a trademark early?

Because early registration is the foundation of protection. We run an availability search, file the application and follow it through.
