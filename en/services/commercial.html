Commercial Litigation & the Economic Courts

Are you owed money by a client or partner, or involved in a commercial dispute between companies? We move quickly to protect your rights.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

A large share of commercial disputes in Egypt are heard by the Economic Courts established by Law No. 120 of 2008, which include a preparatory stage before the hearings. We prepare the file properly from the start, represent you in recovery and litigation, and assess with you whether a settlement would be faster and less costly.

What We Do

  • Recovery of commercial debts and claims between companies.
  • Disputes between partners and shareholders and over company management.
  • Cheque and commercial paper cases.
  • Bankruptcy, preventive composition and restructuring (Law No. 11 of 2018).
  • Trademark and unfair competition disputes.
  • Representation before the Economic Courts at all levels.

How We Work with You

We begin with a consultation, in person or remotely, followed by a written opinion on your position, then a written fee agreement defining the scope, and periodic written reports as the file progresses. See the full engagement steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which courts hear commercial disputes in Egypt?

A large share are heard by the Economic Courts established by Law No. 120 of 2008, which include a preparation stage before hearings.

Yes — bankruptcy, preventive composition and restructuring under Law No. 11 of 2018.
