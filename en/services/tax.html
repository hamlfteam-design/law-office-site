Tax Disputes & Appeals

Facing an inflated tax assessment, an administrative seizure, or a tax claim you dispute? We handle the legal and judicial side of tax disputes, with tax experts working under our direct supervision.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Every tax dispute has two sides: an accounting side (books, returns and figures) and a legal and judicial side (appeals, procedure and litigation). The firm handles the legal and judicial side itself, supported by legal accountants and tax experts working under its direct supervision, while the accounting work as such is carried out by partner accountants outside the firm.

What We Do

  • Challenging tax assessments and claims within the statutory time limits.
  • Representation before the tax appeal committees and dispute-settlement committees.
  • Filing and pursuing tax lawsuits before the courts at all levels.
  • Objecting to administrative seizures and collection measures.
  • Defence in tax evasion cases.
  • Pre-dispute review of a company's tax position, in coordination with its accountants.

How We Work with You

We start with a consultation in person or remotely and a review of the notices and documents, followed by a written opinion on your position and the applicable deadlines, then a written fee agreement defining the scope, and regular written reports. See the full steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does the firm prepare accounts and tax returns?

The accounting side is handled by partner accountants outside the firm. The firm itself handles the legal and judicial side: tax appeals and disputes before the appeal committees and the courts.

I received an inflated tax assessment — what should I do?

Do not wait. Tax appeals are subject to strict time limits, and once they lapse the assessment may become final. Send us the notice and your documents so we can determine the next step in time.

Do you handle corporate tax or only individuals?

Both: companies, associations and institutions, as well as individuals and those carrying on commercial or professional activities.

Need advice on your situation?

Send us a summary of the facts and any documents you have, and we will explain your next step — in English or Arabic.
