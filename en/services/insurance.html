Social Insurance, Pensions & Insurance Claims

Has your pension or insurance entitlement been miscalculated, or has an insurer refused to pay your claim? We review the file and pursue what you are owed.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

The Social Insurance and Pensions Law No. 148 of 2019 sets out how to object to the Authority's decisions before going to court, and private insurance policies carry their own conditions and deadlines for notice and claims. We review the documents or the policy and pursue the appropriate grievance or lawsuit.

What We Do

  • Disputes over the calculation of pensions and insurance entitlements.
  • Insurance registration and proof of periods of service.
  • Grievances against decisions of the National Social Insurance Authority before the dispute review committees.
  • Claims against insurance companies (accident, fire, property, life).
  • Challenging refusal or delay in paying insurance compensation.
  • Reviewing insurance policies before you sign.

Frequently Asked Questions

My pension was calculated incorrectly — what can I do?

Social Insurance and Pensions Law No. 148 of 2019 sets out how to object to the authority's decisions before going to court, including a grievance before the dispute-review committees.

The insurer refused to pay compensation — can I challenge it?

Yes. We claim compensation and challenge refusals or delays. Private insurance policies have notice and claim conditions and deadlines that must be observed.

Do you review insurance policies before signing?

Yes, reviewing insurance policies before contracting is one of our services.
