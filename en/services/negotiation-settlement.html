Negotiation & Amicable Dispute Settlement

Not every dispute has to reach court. If a settlement can protect your rights and save time and money, we negotiate for you and put the agreement in an enforceable form.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Litigation can take years, and a business or family relationship can lose more than the value of the dispute itself. We first give you a frank assessment of your legal position, so you negotiate knowing your strengths, then represent you in negotiation or mediation and draft a settlement agreement that is clear and enforceable.

In many cases, disputes with a government body must first go before the Conciliation Committees established by Law No. 7 of 2000 before a lawsuit is filed.

What We Do

  • A written assessment of your legal position, strengths and weaknesses before negotiation.
  • Negotiating on your behalf with the other party or its counsel.
  • Representation in mediation and amicable settlement sessions.
  • Drafting settlement agreements and releases, and having them recorded by the court where needed.
  • Representation before the Conciliation Committees in disputes with government bodies.
  • Settling disputes between partners, heirs and contracting parties.

How We Work with You

We begin with a consultation, in person or remotely, followed by a written opinion on your position, then a written fee agreement defining the scope, and periodic written reports as the file progresses. See the full engagement steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Lawsuits can take years, and a business or family relationship can lose more than the value of the dispute itself. We first give you a candid assessment of your legal position so you negotiate knowing your strength.

In many cases it must first go before the conciliation committees under Law No. 7 of 2000 before a lawsuit is filed.
