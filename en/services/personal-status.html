Personal Status & Family Law

Family cases are sensitive and call for experience and discretion. We handle your file confidentially, whether you are in Egypt or living abroad.

Contact us about this service

The Idea

Personal status cases are heard by the Family Courts and usually pass through a settlement stage before the lawsuit. Our role is to explain your rights and obligations, try to reach a solution that puts the children's interests first, and, if no amicable solution is possible, represent you before the courts at every level.

What We Do

  • Divorce, khula and judicial divorce for harm.
  • All types of maintenance and enforcement of maintenance judgments.
  • Custody, visitation, hosting and guardianship over property.
  • Registration of marriages between Egyptians and foreign nationals and related procedures.
  • Recognition of foreign divorce judgments and their effect in Egypt.
  • Representing Egyptians living abroad under a formal power of attorney. Learn about power of attorney from abroad.

How We Work with You

We begin with a consultation, in person or remotely, followed by a written opinion on your position, then a written fee agreement defining the scope, and periodic written reports as the file progresses. See the full engagement steps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which courts hear family cases in Egypt?

The Family Courts. These cases usually go through a settlement stage before the lawsuit is filed.

I live abroad — can I file a family case in Egypt?

Yes. We represent Egyptians living abroad under a formal power of attorney, follow the file and send you written reports.

What family matters do you handle?

Divorce, khul' and judicial divorce for harm; maintenance and its enforcement; custody, visitation and guardianship over property; registration of marriages between Egyptians and foreigners; and recognition of foreign divorce judgments in Egypt.

Need advice on your situation?

Send us a summary of the facts and any documents you have, and we will explain your next step — in English or Arabic.
