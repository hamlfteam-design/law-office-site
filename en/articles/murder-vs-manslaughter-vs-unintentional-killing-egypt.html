What is the difference between manslaughter by negligence, murder, and unintentional killing?

By Counselor Dr. Hassan Abdel Moneim Hassan, Attorney before the Court of Cassation

What is the difference between manslaughter by negligence, murder, and unintentional killing?

Many people ask us: “difference between accidental killing and murder”, “unintentional killing”, “penalty for killing by mistake”, “penalty for murder”, and “premeditation and lying in wait”. To answer, we say: Egyptian law has no offence called “unintentional killing”. People mean either battery resulting in death (Article 236), where someone beats another without intent to kill and the victim dies, or killing by mistake (Article 238), where negligence or recklessness causes a death. Murder requires the intent to take life; its penalty is life or aggravated imprisonment and reaches death where there is premeditation, lying in wait or poisoning. How is the offence committed?

Murder arises from an act that causes death with intent to kill, and the court draws the intent from the circumstances, such as the type of instrument, the location of the injury and the number of blows. Premeditation is the settled intent formed before the act after calm thought; lying in wait is waiting for the victim in one or more places for a period to surprise him. Battery resulting in death arises from an intentional assault without intent to kill that ends in death. Killing by mistake arises from fault (negligence, recklessness, lack of caution or failure to observe laws and regulations) that causes a death through a causal link, as in road accidents, medical negligence and workplace accidents.

What should I do to avoid getting involved in something like this?

  • Stay away from fights and do not respond to provocation, and do not carry a weapon or tool in any dispute.
  • Do not drive while under the influence of alcohol or drugs or when exhausted, and observe traffic and speed rules.
  • Follow the standards of your profession and the safety procedures at work; a grave breach increases the penalty.
  • If an accident occurs, stop, help the injured person and call an ambulance immediately; failing to help when able to is an aggravating factor.
  • Do not leave weapons or dangerous substances within reach of others or children.

What are my rights if I am accused of something like this?

  • Do not sign any paper or record.

What is the penalty for this offence?

OffencePenalty
Plain murder (Art. 234)Life imprisonment or aggravated imprisonment.
Murder with premeditation, lying in wait or by poisoning (Arts. 230, 231, 233)Death.
Murder accompanied by another felony (Art. 234, para. 2)Death.
Murder connected with a misdemeanour (Art. 234, para. 3)Death or life imprisonment.
Battery resulting in death (Art. 236)Aggravated imprisonment of 3 to 7 years, harsher if premeditated or lying in wait.
Killing by mistake (Art. 238)Imprisonment of not less than 6 months and a fine not exceeding EGP 200, or either. In the aggravated cases (grave breach of professional standards, being under the influence of alcohol or drugs, and failing to help the injured person) imprisonment of 1 to 5 years and a fine of EGP 100 to 500, or either. Where more than three people die, imprisonment of 1 to 7 years, and 1 to 10 years if an aggravating factor also applies.

For a death sentence, judgment may be issued only by the unanimous vote of the court's members after obtaining the opinion of the Grand Mufti, and the case must be referred to the Court of Cassation.

Does a pardon or early release apply to this penalty, or does the accused serve it in full?

It depends on the circumstances of the case, the penal provisions stated in the conviction judgment, and the decisions of the Minister of Interior in this regard.
