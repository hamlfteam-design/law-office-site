What is the difference between pretrial detention and a prison sentence in Egypt, and how long can pretrial detention last?

By Counselor Dr. Hassan Abdelmoneim Hassan

What is the difference between pretrial detention and a prison sentence in Egypt, and how long can pretrial detention last?

Pretrial detention is a temporary measure ordered by the prosecution or a judge while a case is pending and before judgment, and the accused remains presumed innocent throughout. A prison sentence can only be imposed by a court judgment that convicts the accused and sets the term. Pretrial detention has a ceiling that cannot be exceeded, which depends on the type of offence (misdemeanour, felony, or felony punishable by life imprisonment or death), and it is governed by the new Code of Criminal Procedure, Law No. 174 of 2025, in force since 1 October 2026.

What exactly is the difference?

Why can someone be detained before any judgment?

Pretrial detention exists to protect the investigation, not to punish: for example where there is a risk of flight, of pressure on witnesses or tampering with evidence, or a threat to public security. It is meant to be the exception, with alternatives used whenever they are enough.

The cases in which detention is permitted, and the article that sets them out in the new Code, need to be checked against the official text [needs verification].

What is the maximum length of pretrial detention?

According to the text of Article 123 as approved by the House of Representatives in January 2025 during debate on the draft Code, pretrial detention during the preliminary investigation may not exceed:

  • four months for misdemeanours;
  • twelve months for felonies;
  • eighteen months where the prescribed penalty is life imprisonment or death.

These figures come from a press report on the draft stage, so the article number and wording must be checked against Law No. 174 of 2025 as published in the Official Gazette [needs verification].

Press reports on the new Code also say the case file is referred to the Public Prosecutor every three months of detention, or since the last review, to assess whether detention should continue.

Is time in pretrial detention deducted from the sentence?

The established rule in Egyptian law is that time spent in pretrial detention is deducted from the sentence when it is enforced. The article stating this in the new Code needs to be confirmed [needs verification]. Compensation for pretrial detention where the case ends in acquittal or a decision not to prosecute must be checked against the official text before it is relied on [needs verification].

What should I do if a relative is in pretrial detention?

  • Track the renewal hearing dates and make sure a lawyer attends each one.
  • Provide proof of residence, employment, health and family circumstances with the release application.
  • Count the time already served; reaching the ceiling requires release under the law.

Specialist legal opinion

Under the new Code, pretrial detention is narrower in its periods and broader in its alternatives, which gives the defence more room at renewal hearings: asking for an alternative measure instead of custody, relying on the expiry of the ceiling, and arguing that the grounds for detention no longer exist once the investigation is complete. How these rules apply to cases that began before 1 October 2026 depends on the Code's transitional provisions, which need to be reviewed [needs verification].

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between pretrial detention and a prison sentence?

Pretrial detention is a temporary measure during investigation or trial while the person is presumed innocent; a prison sentence follows a conviction.

What is the maximum length of pretrial detention in Egypt?

According to Article 123 as approved by the House in January 2025: four months for misdemeanours, twelve months for felonies and eighteen months where the penalty is life imprisonment or death, during the preliminary investigation. These figures must be checked against the officially published text.

Is pretrial detention deducted from the sentence?

The established rule is that it is deducted when the sentence is enforced; the article in the new Code needs to be confirmed.

Can someone be released before the ceiling is reached?

Yes, at any renewal hearing, on bail, under an alternative measure or unconditionally, if the competent authority so decides.

When did the new Code of Criminal Procedure take effect?

Law No. 174 of 2025 was issued and published on 12 November 2025 and has been in force since 1 October 2026.
