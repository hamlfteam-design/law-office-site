What is the penalty for drug trafficking and smuggling? Is there any chance of a reduced sentence?

By Counselor Dr. Hassan Abdel Moneim Hassan, Attorney before the Court of Cassation

What is the penalty for drug trafficking and smuggling? Is there any chance of a reduced sentence?

Many people ask us: “penalty for drug trafficking”, “penalty for drug smuggling”, “Article 33 of the narcotics law”, “Article 34 of the narcotics law”, and “I was accused of trafficking in drugs”. To answer, we say: trafficking in and smuggling drugs are among the most severely punished crimes in Egyptian law. Smuggling (importing or exporting a narcotic without a licence) is punishable by death and a fine of EGP 100,000 to 500,000 (Article 33), and possession with intent to traffic or trafficking in any form is punishable by death or life imprisonment and the same fine (Article 34). The judge may apply leniency only down to the next penalty and not below 6 years (Article 36). To understand your rights, stay with us and read these questions and answers.

What do trafficking and smuggling mean legally?

Smuggling is importing or exporting a narcotic without a licence; trafficking is dealing in a narcotic by selling, buying, transporting, delivering or possessing it with intent to traffic. Also treated as trafficking are supplying a narcotic for use in return for payment, running a place for drug use in return for payment, and a licensed holder disposing of a narcotic for a purpose other than the licensed one.

How is the offence committed?

The offence arises from one of the acts listed in Articles 33 and 34, with knowledge of the nature of the narcotic and intent to traffic or smuggle; intent is drawn from the circumstances and evidence of the case. The law increases the penalty in specific circumstances, including: involving, or selling to, a person under 21; the offender being an official in charge of combating narcotics or abusing his official authority; the offence occurring in or next to places of worship, education, hospitals, clubs, public gardens or prisons; or the substance being cocaine or heroin.

What should I do to avoid getting involved in something like this?

  • Do not receive, carry or deliver any shipment, parcel or bag when you do not know its contents, even from someone you trust or for an attractive fee.
  • Do not allow your name, account, address, car or storeroom to be used to receive or store items for others.
  • Do not sign for goods or shipments before verifying their source and contents.
  • Stay away from any anonymous job or offer that is unusually profitable.
  • Do not carry controlled medicines when travelling except with the original prescription and after learning the customs rules.

What are my rights if I am accused of something like this?

  • Do not sign any paper or record.
  • Do not say a single word except in the presence of your lawyer.

This article is based on the laws currently in force, including the Code of Criminal Procedure currently in force; the new Law No. 174 of 2025 has been postponed until 1 October 2027.

What is the penalty for this offence?

Article and offencePenalty
Art. 33: importing or exporting a narcotic without a licence, producing it for trade, cultivating prohibited plants for trade, and forming, running or joining a drug-trade gangDeath and a fine of EGP 100,000 to 500,000, with customs compensation.
Art. 34: possession, sale, transport or delivery with intent to traffic, a licensed holder disposing of it for another purpose, and running a place for use in return for paymentDeath or life imprisonment and a fine of EGP 100,000 to 500,000; death in the aggravated cases.
Art. 36: limit on leniencyThe judge may reduce the penalty only to the next penalty down, and not below 6 years where that next penalty is aggravated imprisonment or imprisonment.

For a death sentence, judgment may be issued only by the unanimous vote of the court's members after obtaining the opinion of the Grand Mufti, and the case must be referred to the Court of Cassation.

Does a pardon or early release apply to this penalty, or does the accused serve it in full?

It depends on the circumstances of the case, the penal provisions stated in the conviction judgment, and the decisions of the Minister of Interior in this regard.
