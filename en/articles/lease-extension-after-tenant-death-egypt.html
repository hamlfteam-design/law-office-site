Does a Lease Pass On After the Tenant’s Death? Who Benefits from the Statutory Extension? And What Changed Under Law No. 164 of 2025?

By Counselor Ali El-Shennawy

Does a Lease Pass On After the Tenant’s Death? Who Benefits from the Statutory Extension? And What Changed Under Law No. 164 of 2025?

Many people ask us: “Is an old-rent lease inherited?”, “Who may stay in the apartment after the tenant dies?”, “My son is married and lives with me — does the lease extend to him?”, “Will my old-rent lease end or continue?” To answer: Under the Civil Code, a lease is a financial right that does not end with the tenant’s death. The statutory extension in old-rent leases, however, belongs to the persons the law specifies, on condition of stable residence. Since Law No. 164 of 2025, it is subject to a transitional period after which it ends: seven years for residential and five for non-residential premises.

What is the extension of a lease?

It is the continuation of the lease on its same terms, after the death of the original tenant or his abandonment of the premises, for the benefit of those the law designates, without a new contract or the landlord’s consent.

What is the difference between a Civil Code lease and an old-rent lease upon the tenant’s death?

Point of comparisonLease governed by the Civil CodeOld-rent lease (Laws No. 49 of 1977 and No. 136 of 1981)
Effect of deathThe lease does not end with the death of the tenant or the lessor and passes to the heirs (Civil Code, Art. 601)The lease extends for the benefit of those designated by law (Art. 29 of Law No. 49 of 1977)
TerminationThe heirs may request termination if they prove that its burdens have become heavier than their means, or that it was concluded in consideration of the tenant’s personThe landlord may terminate only in the cases specified by law

Who benefits from the extension in residential leases?

Those enumerated in Article 29 of Law No. 49 of 1977 among the original tenant’s relatives who resided with him on a stable basis, such as the spouse, children and parents. The court verifies the capacity of each claimant and the degree of kinship to the tenant, and the Court of Cassation has quashed judgments that failed to examine this defence.

What is stable residence? Do marriage or absence affect it?

The Court of Cassation has settled that the residence that counts is stable residence with the original tenant until his death or his abandonment of the dwelling, without requiring subsequent residence. The absence of the beneficiary for a transient reason (such as marriage) is not an abandonment of the lease unless he expressly or impliedly reveals his intention to that effect.

What about non-residential premises and furnished lets?

Extension of a lease of non-residential premises (shop, office, clinic) is affected by actual continuation of the activity in the premises, and each case has its own details and should be reviewed by a specialist. A furnished let, by contrast, is not subject to the statutory extension rules of old-rent leases and is governed by the Civil Code.

What changed after Law No. 164 of 2025?

The law came into force on 5 August 2025 and ended the open-ended extension. Laws No. 49 of 1977 and No. 136 of 1981 remain in force during the transitional period, so extension continues for those who meet its conditions until seven years have elapsed for residential premises (approximately August 2032) and five years for non-residential premises leased to natural persons (approximately August 2030). The old laws are then repealed, the lease ends, and the tenant or the person to whom it extended must vacate. The parties may agree on an earlier termination.

What should I do to avoid trouble?

Keep the lease contract, the rent receipts, and documents proving your stable residence with the original tenant (official papers and bills). Do not surrender the premises or vacate them before presenting your position to a specialized lawyer.

A question you may want answered

Question: Does a person to whom an old-rent lease extends need the landlord’s consent or a new contract?

Answer: No. The lease extends on its same terms to those who meet the conditions of extension, without a new contract or the landlord’s consent, during the transitional period fixed by Law No. 164 of 2025.
