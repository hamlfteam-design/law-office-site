Can My Employer Dismiss Me? What Are My Rights in Unfair Dismissal Under the New Labor Law No. 14 of 2025?

By Counselor Ahmed Shawky Helal

Can My Employer Dismiss Me? What Are My Rights in Unfair Dismissal Under the New Labor Law No. 14 of 2025?

Many people ask us: “I was dismissed without any reason — what do I do?”, “My employer gave me a warning — is that allowed?”, “How much is the compensation for unfair dismissal?” and “Can he dismiss me without an investigation?” To answer: Neither the employer nor the employee may terminate an open-ended employment contract except for a legitimate and sufficient reason and on three months’ prior written notice; and if the employer terminates it for an illegitimate reason, the employee is entitled to compensation of not less than two months’ wages for each year of service, without prejudice to his other rights (Articles 156, 157 and 165 of Labor Law No. 14 of 2025).

What is unfair dismissal?

It is the employer’s termination of an open-ended employment contract for an illegitimate reason. The law lists among illegitimate reasons: membership of a trade-union organization, filing a complaint against the employer, using statutory leave, and discrimination on grounds such as colour, sex, marital status, pregnancy, religion or political opinion (Art. 165).

Is written notice alone enough for my employer to dismiss me?

No. Written notice three months before termination is a requirement (Art. 156), but it does not replace the need for a legitimate and sufficient reason (Art. 157). If the employer terminates the contract without notice, or before the notice period has run, he must pay the employee an amount equal to his wages for the notice period or its remaining part (Art. 164).

What is the difference between the three situations of termination by the employer of an open-ended contract?

SituationConsequenceReference
Termination for a legitimate and sufficient reason, on three months’ prior written noticeValid termination of the contractArts. 156 and 157
Termination without notice or before the notice period has runAn amount equal to the wages for the notice period or its remaining partArt. 164
Termination for an illegitimate reasonCompensation of not less than two months’ wages for each year of service, without prejudice to other rightsArt. 165

Can I be dismissed as a disciplinary penalty without an investigation?

No penalty may be imposed on an employee except after he has been notified in writing of the allegations and his statements have been heard, and the decision must state its reasons (Art. 141); the penalty must also be proportionate to the violation (Art. 138). As for the penalty of dismissal, jurisdiction to impose it lies with the competent labor court (Art. 148).

What if I stay away from work? Is that treated as resignation?

An employee is deemed to have resigned if he is absent without a justification for more than twenty intermittent days in a year or more than ten consecutive days, after being warned by registered letter (Art. 166). Each case has its own details and should be reviewed by a specialist.

What should I do to avoid trouble?

Keep your employment contract, whatever proves your wages and length of service, and any written notice or decision you receive from the employer. Do not sign, do not endorse, and do not say a word in any investigation except in the presence of your lawyer.

What are the procedures for bringing the dispute?

Either party may, within ten days of the dispute arising, request its amicable settlement before the competent committee, which must conclude its work within twenty-one days of the request (Art. 149). If no settlement is reached, the dispute is referred to the labor court, which decides a dismissal claim on an expedited basis within three months of the first hearing (Art. 150). Each claim has its own time limit, so bring your problem to a specialist as soon as it arises.

A question you may want answered

Question: What is the minimum compensation if I am dismissed for an illegitimate reason?

Answer: The compensation is not less than two months’ wages for each year of service, without prejudice to the employee’s other rights (Art. 165 of Labor Law No. 14 of 2025).
