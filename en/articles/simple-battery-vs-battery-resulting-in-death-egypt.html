What is the difference between simple battery and battery resulting in death? When does an ordinary fight become a felony?

By Counselor Dr. Hassan Abdel Moneim Hassan, Attorney before the Court of Cassation

What is the difference between simple battery and battery resulting in death? When does an ordinary fight become a felony?

Many people ask us: “difference between simple battery and battery resulting in death”, “penalty for battery resulting in death”, “penalty for simple assault”, “I hit him and he died”, and “a fight in which someone died”. To answer, we say: simple battery is a misdemeanour punishable by imprisonment of up to one year or a fine (Article 242), whereas battery resulting in death is a felony punishable by aggravated imprisonment (Article 236). The difference is that in the second, the assault ends with the victim's death without the offender intending to kill. To understand your rights, stay with us and read these questions and answers.

What is simple battery and what is battery resulting in death?

Simple batteryBattery resulting in death
ArticleArt. 242, Penal CodeArt. 236, Penal Code
MeaningBeating or wounding that does not reach the seriousness set out in the two preceding Articles, i.e. it leaves no illness or incapacity for personal work exceeding 20 daysIntentionally beating, wounding or administering harmful substances, without intent to kill, that results in the victim's death
Type of offenceMisdemeanourFelony
Offender's intentTo assault physicallyTo assault physically, without intent to take life

How is the offence committed?

In both cases the act is an intentional assault (beating, wounding or administering a harmful substance), and the classification depends on the result: a minor injury makes it simple battery; death caused by the beating makes it battery resulting in death. Death need not be immediate; the offender is liable for the death if his act caused it, even if other factors such as the victim's poor health contributed. If it is proved that the offender intended to kill, the offence becomes murder, and the court draws that intent from the circumstances, such as the type of instrument, the location of the injury and the number of blows.

What should I do to avoid getting involved in something like this?

  • Stay away from fights and do not respond to provocation; an ordinary fight can end in a death in a moment.
  • Do not use a weapon, stick or tool in any dispute; using them increases the penalty.
  • Do not strike the head or sensitive parts of the body.
  • If someone is hurt, help them, call an ambulance immediately and do not flee the scene.
  • Resolve your disputes through legal channels: a report, a complaint or a lawsuit.

What are my rights if I am accused of something like this?

  • Do not sign any paper or record.
  • Do not say a single word except in the presence of your lawyer.

This article is based on the laws currently in force, including the Code of Criminal Procedure currently in force; the new Law No. 174 of 2025 has been postponed until 1 October 2027.

What is the penalty for this offence?

OffencePenalty
Simple beating or wounding (Art. 242)Imprisonment of up to one year or the fine prescribed by the text; up to two years or the fine if premeditated or lying in wait; and imprisonment if weapons, sticks, machines or tools are used.
Beating or wounding causing illness or incapacity exceeding 20 days (Art. 241)Imprisonment of up to two years or the prescribed fine; imprisonment if premeditated, lying in wait or using a tool.
Battery resulting in death (Art. 236)Aggravated imprisonment of 3 to 7 years, with a harsher penalty if premeditated or lying in wait; it is a felony.

Does a pardon or early release apply to this penalty, or does the accused serve it in full?

It depends on the circumstances of the case, the penal provisions stated in the conviction judgment, and the decisions of the Minister of Interior in this regard.
