FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Concise answers to the legal questions people ask most, with their legal basis. Every case has its own facts, so these answers do not replace a consultation with a lawyer on your file.

What is the time limit for filing a cassation appeal against a criminal judgment in Egypt?

Answer: Sixty days from the date of the judgment rendered in the presence of the accused, under Article 34 of Law No. 57 of 1959 on the Cases and Procedures of Appeal before the Court of Cassation, as amended by Law No. 74 of 2007. The memorandum of grounds must be filed within the same period; otherwise, the appeal is dismissed as inadmissible in form. This rule applies until the new Code of Criminal Procedure (Law No. 174 of 2025) enters into force on 2027/10/01.

What is the time limit for filing a cassation appeal against a civil judgment?

Answer: Sixty days from the date the judgment is rendered, under Article 252 of the Code of Civil and Commercial Procedure.

A misdemeanour judgment was issued against me in absentia. What can I do?

Answer: You may file an opposition (objection) against the judgment within ten days of being served with it, under Article 398 of the Code of Criminal Procedure. If the opposition is dismissed, the time limit for appeal is ten days from the date of the judgment rendered on the opposition, under Article 406 of the same Code. This applies until the new Code of Criminal Procedure enters into force on 2027/10/01.

I am being blackmailed or threatened online. What should I do?

Answer: Do not pay and do not delete the conversations. Keep screenshots and the links to the accounts involved, then file a complaint with the Public Prosecution or the General Department for Combating Information Technology Crimes. These acts are punishable under Law No. 175 of 2018 on Combating Information Technology Crimes and Article 327 of the Penal Code (threats).

I live outside Egypt. How can I grant a power of attorney to a lawyer in Egypt?

Answer: Through the Egyptian consulate in your country of residence. Alternatively, the power of attorney may be notarised by the local notary authority, then legalised by that country's Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Egyptian embassy, and finally by the Egyptian Ministry of Foreign Affairs in Egypt.

How do I obtain a declaration of heirs (I'lam Wiratha)? What is the penalty for withholding an inheritance?

Answer: A declaration of heirs is obtained by filing an application with the competent Family Court. Intentionally refusing to hand over an heir's lawful share is a criminal offence punishable under Article 49 of Inheritance Law No. 77 of 1943, added by Law No. 219 of 2017.

Can a foreigner own real estate in Egypt?

Answer: Yes. Under Law No. 230 of 1996, a foreigner may own up to two properties for residential purposes, each not exceeding 4,000 square metres, and may not dispose of them within five years of acquisition, subject to the exceptions set out in the law.

How is a foreign judgment enforced in Egypt?

Answer: By filing an action for an enforcement order (exequatur) before the Court of First Instance, under Articles 296 to 298 of the Code of Civil and Commercial Procedure. Key conditions include reciprocity, the judgment being final, the parties having been duly summoned, and the judgment not conflicting with a prior Egyptian judgment or with public order.

Is the legal consultation free of charge?

Answer: No. Consultations are paid, whether held in person at the office or by video call. Fees and duration are shown when you book on the website.

When can I meet a lawyer?

Answer: Client meetings are held on Sundays and Tuesdays, subject to available slots, and are booked online through the website.
