Buying Property in Egypt

A foreigner, expat or Arab buyer considering property in Egypt? We verify the file before you pay, and follow the registration on your behalf.

The Idea

Buying property from abroad means trusting documents you cannot check yourself. Our role is to be your eyes inside Egypt: we examine the property, the seller and the contract before any transfer of money, and we follow registration and transfer of ownership until the file is closed.

The rules on ownership of property in Egypt by non-Egyptians carry conditions and restrictions that vary by the type and location of the property and the status of the buyer. We check them at the start of the file, before any financial commitment.

What We Do

  • Examine the chain of title and the legal status of the property, including any rights or encumbrances attached to it.
  • Review the purchase contract and draft its terms to protect the buyer, especially payment, delivery and warranty terms.
  • Follow registration and transfer-of-ownership procedures before the competent authorities.
  • Represent you under an official power of attorney if you cannot attend, without traveling. Learn about power of attorney from abroad.
  • Handle sale, inheritance and disputes connected to the property, if the file becomes complicated or a disagreement arises after purchase.

How We Work with You

We begin with a remote consultation, followed by a written opinion on the property and the contract, then a written fee agreement defining the scope. After that you receive periodic written reports in Arabic or English on every step. See the full engagement steps.

Company Formation & Foreign Investment in Egypt

A foreign or Arab investor entering the Egyptian market? We choose the right legal form with you and carry the formation through from the first step.

The Idea

Most mistakes by foreign investors happen on day one: the wrong legal form, an incomplete partners' agreement, or a license that was never checked. We start by understanding your business and objective, then choose the structure, prepare the papers and follow the authorities inside Egypt on your behalf.

The suitable legal form and the licensing authority vary with the activity, the location, the nationality of the partners and the ownership percentages. We cannot fix them before understanding your project, so the first consultation focuses on exactly that.

What We Do

  • Choose the legal form of the project (limited liability company, joint-stock company, branch of a foreign company, representative office and others) according to the activity and purpose.
  • Prepare the articles of association and the shareholder or partner agreements to protect your rights.
  • Follow formation and licensing procedures with the competent authorities, including the investment authority where the nature of the project requires it.
  • Review the company's first commercial contracts: supply, lease, employment and dealings with suppliers and customers.
  • Stay with you after formation through the monthly legal subscription for companies: contracts, labor, collection and periodic advice.

How We Work with You

A first remote consultation, then a written opinion on the proposed structure, then a written fee agreement defining the scope, then execution inside Egypt with periodic reports in Arabic or English. See the full engagement steps.

International Commercial Arbitration & Trade Contracts

Trading with a party in Egypt in import, export or supply? A well-drafted contract from the start saves you a long dispute later.

The Idea

Most international trade disputes start with an arbitration clause written in a hurry, or a contract that never fixed the governing law or the place of decision. Before contracting we help you with careful drafting; after a dispute we give strong representation before the arbitral tribunal or the court; and after the award we enforce it inside Egypt.

The choice of arbitration centre, its seat and the governing law are decisions that shape the whole dispute. They are best made before signing, not after the disagreement begins.

What We Do

  • Draft and review the arbitration clause: the centre, seat, language, number of arbitrators and governing law.
  • Draft import, export, trade and supply contracts, and review contracts offered to you by the other side.
  • Represent parties in arbitral proceedings, inside or outside Egypt, and prepare the memoranda and evidence.
  • Enforce arbitral awards inside Egypt. Learn about enforcing foreign judgments and awards.
  • Collect trade receivables and negotiate settlement before arbitration, where settlement serves you better.

How We Work with You

A first remote consultation, then a study of the contract and documents with a written opinion, then a written fee agreement, then follow-up of the file with periodic reports in Arabic or English. See the full engagement steps.

Criminal Defense for Foreigners in Egypt

A relative of yours is a foreign national arrested or accused in a criminal case in Egypt, and you are far away? We represent them before the Public Prosecution and the courts, and keep the family and the embassy informed in writing.

The Idea

When a foreign national is arrested or questioned in Egypt, the family is usually in another country, unfamiliar with the system, the language and the procedures. Our role is to be the lawyer on the ground for them: attending the investigation, pleading before the Criminal Courts and the Court of Cassation, and reporting every development clearly and in their language.

Contact with us starts from the accused's family, or from the embassy or consulate of their country (the citizen affairs section). We do not initiate contact with the accused or their relatives.

What Happens After an Arrest (in General)

  • The accused is brought before the Public Prosecution for investigation.
  • The Prosecution decides on release or pretrial detention; where detention is ordered, there are procedures for appeal and for renewal of detention.
  • After the investigation, if charges are referred to the competent court (including the Criminal Courts for the more serious offenses), trial hearings are set.
  • After the judgment, an appeal before the Court of Cassation may be filed, subject to the legal conditions and deadlines.

Rights of the Accused (in Principle)

  • Being assisted by a lawyer during the investigation.
  • Contacting their family and lawyer, a right guaranteed by the Egyptian Constitution to anyone whose liberty is restricted.
  • Having an interpreter if they do not speak Arabic.
  • Having their country's consulate notified of the arrest, under the Vienna Convention on Consular Relations.

What We Do

  • Attend the investigation with the accused and present the defense from the very first stage.
  • Apply for release, or appeal pretrial detention orders.
  • Plead before the Criminal Courts, and appeal to the Court of Cassation after judgment.
  • Arrange the necessary power of attorney, especially when the family is outside Egypt. Learn about powers of attorney from abroad.
  • Follow the case with periodic written reports to the family and the embassy concerned, in Arabic or English, with the accused's consent.

How We Work

The family or the embassy sends us the accused's details, place of detention and any available case papers. We review the file and identify the first urgent step, then a written fee agreement defines the scope, followed by periodic reports on every hearing and development. See the full working process.

Individuals' Disputes with Real Estate Developers

Bought a unit from a developer and delivery is late, the unit does not match the contract, or you want to rescind and recover your money? We review your contract and identify the strongest legal route.

The Idea

The relationship between an individual buyer and a real estate developer is usually governed by a standard-form contract drafted by the developer, with terms that tend to favor it. Many buyers discover the problem only after years of paying installments: late delivery, specifications that differ from what was agreed, price increases, or a stalled project. Our role is to read the contract and documents carefully, explain your rights and options, and represent you before the competent forum.

Common Disputes We Handle

  • Delay in delivering the unit beyond the agreed date.
  • Delivery of a unit that deviates from the area, specifications or finishes stated in the contract or brochure.
  • Price increases or additional charges (maintenance, owners' association, fees) not provided for in the contract.
  • A stalled or distressed project.
  • Rescission (termination) of the contract and restitution of the amounts paid (down payment and installments).
  • Claims for damages for loss caused by breach of contract.
  • Actions to declare the sale contract valid and enforceable and to register title to the unit in the buyer's name.
  • Arbitration clauses found in some developers' contracts, and how to deal with them.

What We Do

  • Review the contract, all receipts and correspondence, and assess your legal position.
  • Serve a formal notice on the developer through a court bailiff, demanding performance or placing it in default, before going to court.
  • Negotiate and settle amicably where that serves your interest.
  • File the appropriate claim before the competent court, or represent you before the arbitral tribunal where the contract contains an arbitration clause.
  • Engage an engineering expert to document defects in the unit or the project where needed (including through a court-appointed expert).
  • Rely on the provisions of the Consumer Protection Law in cases where it applies.
Documents that help us at the first session: the reservation or sale contract, payment receipts, any correspondence or messages with the developer, the brochure or advertisements you relied on, and the installment schedule.
