WHO WE ARE

About

Instead of building a legal department… leave it to us.

A law firm in Downtown Cairo. We represent companies and individuals before all levels of courts, including the Court of Cassation. Lawyers, consultants, legal accountants and tax experts under one roof.

For companies: Contracts, labor matters, debt collection, tax, litigation before the economic courts, and a monthly legal subscription.

For foreign and Arab clients: Attorney and correspondent counsel inside Egypt — entity formation, investment protection, real estate and inheritance, enforcement of judgments and arbitration. Periodic reports in Arabic and English.

For individuals: Criminal cases and Cassation appeals, civil and commercial matters, personal status.

Our Way of Working

  • We study the file before giving an opinion — a documented written opinion, not an oral one.
  • A written fee agreement defining the scope before work begins.
  • A periodic written report on the progress of your file — even when there is nothing new.
  • Every opinion issued in the firm's name is approved personally by its head.

FOREIGN & ARAB CLIENTS

For Arabs and Foreigners Living in Egypt or Dealing with It from Abroad

If you came to Egypt and needed a lawyer — whether through your embassy or consulate, or by searching yourself — what wastes the most time and trust is: a lawyer who does not respond quickly, fees with no clear written price, and a file you lose track of while you are outside Egypt. We work the opposite way: direct communication in Arabic or English with the lawyer personally responsible for your file (not a receptionist), a written and clear scope and fees from day one, and periodic written reports — even when there is no new development — so you stay reassured while following from afar.

Direct Representation for Individuals

Criminal and civil cases, contracts, or company disputes — whether the file came to us directly or through the embassy/consulate, or you searched for us yourself.

Correspondent Counsel for Foreign and Arab Law Firms

If your firm has a client who needs legal representation in Egypt — we work as local correspondent counsel: same file, same follow-up standards and periodic reports, under a clear fee agreement (hourly, fixed fee, or referral commission).

Mutual Referral Partnership

For our Egyptian clients who need legal representation abroad, we are building a network of trusted foreign and Arab firms to cooperate with on a reciprocal basis — each party referring its clients to the other firm according to the country required.

Do you have a firm outside Egypt and want to cooperate with us in representing your clients here, or does one of your clients need a lawyer in Egypt? Contact us and we will send you the cooperation details.
