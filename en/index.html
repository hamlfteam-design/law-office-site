What sets our process apart

Clients working across borders consistently need four things that are often missing:

  • Procedural clarity — exactly what step is required, before which authority, and how long it realistically takes.
  • Regular reporting — a written status update on a set cadence, even when there is no new development.
  • Professional English communication — not a translated afterthought.
  • Clear scope — what the engagement covers and what it does not, stated up front.
A general power of attorney does not cover every purpose. Sale, litigation, and inheritance each require their own specific wording — an incorrectly worded POA gets procedures rejected and can cost months of delay. We confirm the correct wording before you go to the consulate.
