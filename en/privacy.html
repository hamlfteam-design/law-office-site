Privacy Policy

Keeping our clients' information confidential is a professional duty before it is a legal obligation. This page explains what data we collect, why, how we protect it, and your rights over it.

Last updated: 13 November 2026

1. Who we are

Dr. Hassan Abdelmoneim Hassan Law Firm — Attorneys & Legal Consultants ("the Firm", "we"), 48 Qasr El Nil Street, Mostafa Kamel Square, Abdeen, Downtown Cairo, Egypt — 6th floor, office 25. The Firm is responsible for the data collected through hamlf.com, the client portal and the Firm's apps, and through our official channels (email info@hamlf.com and the Firm's WhatsApp number +20 120 855 5806).

2. Data we collect

  • Contact details: name, phone number and email address when you use the contact form, book a consultation or write to us.
  • Consultation and case data: the description of your matter, documents, messages and voice notes you send us, and, if you are our client, your cases and hearing details.
  • WhatsApp messages: when you message the Firm's number we receive your number, WhatsApp profile name, the content and attachments of your messages, and the delivery status of our replies.
  • Client portal account data: your email address and the sign-in data needed to show you your cases and documents.
  • Payment data: online consultation fees are paid directly through the payment gateway; we never see or store your card details, only the transaction reference, status and amount.
  • Technical data: general website usage (pages visited, device type, approximate country) through analytics tools, which may use cookies.

3. Why we use your data

  • To answer your enquiries and schedule consultations and video sessions.
  • To study your matter, provide the agreed legal services, follow up on your cases and keep you informed.
  • To give you access to your cases and documents through the client portal.
  • To issue receipts and invoices and meet our legal and tax obligations.
  • To improve the website based on aggregated statistics that do not identify you.

We do not sell your data, use it for targeted advertising, or send you marketing messages without your consent.

4. Professional confidentiality

Everything you share with us about your matter is covered by lawyers' professional secrecy under Egyptian law. Only the members of our team assigned to your file can access it, and we do not disclose it to anyone without your consent or a legal obligation.

5. Service providers

We rely on trusted technology providers who process data on our behalf and only for the purposes above:

  • Hostinger: website and email hosting.
  • Google: Firebase for the client portal and the Firm's apps, Google Drive for document storage, Google Analytics for statistics, and Google Translate when you choose to translate a page.
  • Meta (WhatsApp): receiving and replying to WhatsApp messages through the WhatsApp Business Platform, possibly via a Meta-approved service provider.
  • FormSubmit: delivering "Contact us" form messages to the Firm's mailbox.
  • OPay: processing consultation booking payments.
  • Daily: video consultations.

Some of this data may be processed on servers outside Egypt under these providers' policies and safeguards.

6. Use of digital assistants

We may use AI tools to help our team organise and summarise messages and prepare draft replies. No answer or legal opinion is sent to you before it has been reviewed and approved by the Firm's team.

7. Data retention

We keep data for as long as we deal with you, and afterwards for as long as needed to protect your rights and ours and to meet our legal and professional obligations; we then delete or anonymise it. Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted once they are no longer needed.

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, including encryption in transit (HTTPS), access restricted by roles and passwords, verification of the source of incoming messages, and keeping service keys out of public files. No transmission or storage method is completely secure, but we act on any incident as soon as it is discovered.

9. Your rights

Under Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law No. 151 of 2020, you have the right to:

  • Know what data we hold about you and obtain a copy.
  • Have inaccurate or incomplete data corrected.
  • Request deletion or restriction of your data, unless we are legally required to keep it or need it to protect a right.
  • Withdraw consent for any processing based on consent, and object to processing.
  • Stop receiving our WhatsApp or email messages at any time by telling us.

To exercise any of these rights, write to info@hamlf.com. We will respond within a reasonable time after verifying your identity.

10. Minors

Our services are intended for adults. Where a matter concerns a minor, communication is handled by the minor's legal guardian.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or legal requirements change; the date of the latest update is shown at the top of this page.
